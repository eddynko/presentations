Transient Multiagent Pathfinding (TMAPF)

  • Input: \(k\) agents placed on a graph $G$. An agent $R_i$ has starting position $s_i$ and destination $t_i$.

  • Dynamics: In each time step, each agent may

    • enter the graph at $s_i$,
    • move along an edge without collision,
    • stay at its current position
    • leave graph at $t_i$

  • Goal: Find a schedule for the agents that minimizes Task-completion time (makespan).

TMAPF: Example

Background

  • (T)MAPF has been extensively studied over the last four decades by researchers in computational geometry, artificial intelligence and robotics

  • Annual workshops affiliated with the top AI conferences (e.g., AAAI, IJCAI) dedicated to MAPF

  • Posed as The Symposium on Computational Geometry (SoCG 2021) Challenge

  • Many algorithms, heuristics, websites, benchmarks, etc, dedicated to MAPF

Background

Many settings/variants:

  • Combinatorial: grids, subgrids and graphs

  • Geometric: Euclidean plane w/o obstacles (robots are geometric shapes)

  • Different conflict notions, stay/disappear on target ...

  • Labelled/unlabelled, sliding/translation

Most of the settings are NP-hard even on very restricted instances such as grids and trees.

Makespan upper bound

Lemma: Makespan of any TMAPF istance is at most $1+\sum_{i\in [k]}dist(s_i,t_i)$ .
